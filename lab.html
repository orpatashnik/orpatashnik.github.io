Lab

Computer Graphics · Vision · Generative Models

Research group at Tel Aviv University

The lab is co-directed by Or Patashnik and Daniel Cohen-Or. We work on controllable visual generation, image and video editing, personalization, and methods that make generative models more reliable, expressive, and useful for creative workflows.
